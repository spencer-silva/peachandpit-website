How it works

It's simple.

Once a day, users share their Peach (best part) and their Pit (worst part) — then rate their day on a scale from 1 to 5.

1
Share your Peach What was the best part of your day?
2
Share your Pit What was the hardest part of your day?
3
Add a Stem (optional) The weird part of your day, or anything that's neither a peach nor a pit.
4
Rate your day Score it 1–5 and watch your calendar fill in with color.

More than a journal

Streaks & badges

Post every day to build your streak, and earn badges for milestones along the way.

Stem

Not every moment is a peach or a pit. Add a Stem for the weird, funny or in-between parts.

Photos

Add a photo to any post so you remember what the day actually looked like.

Groups

Share with a smaller circle, like family, roommates or your closest friends.
